# Welcome to the FishATLAS wiki!

FishATLAS is a MATLAB package for mapping and comparing organ-specific patterns of cancer cell dissemination in zebrafish larvae. Using the vasculature as an anatomical reference, it combines imaging data from individual fish into population atlases to identify preferred colonization sites and compare spatial patterns across cancer cell lines and experimental conditions.
